Reciprocal Museums in Virginia

Virginia has 38 museums in the two big reciprocal-admission networks: 9 in the ASTC Travel Passport program (free general admission for science-center members) and 29 in NARM (free member admission at art, history, and children's museums). Open the interactive maps to set your own home museum — they compute which venues the 90-mile and 15/50-mile exclusion rules remove for you.
