Reciprocal Museums in Rhode Island

Rhode Island has 17 museums in the two big reciprocal-admission networks: 1 in the ASTC Travel Passport program (free general admission for science-center members) and 16 in NARM (free member admission at art, history, and children's museums). Open the interactive maps to set your own home museum — they compute which venues the 90-mile and 15/50-mile exclusion rules remove for you.
